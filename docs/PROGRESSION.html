Combine · chapter progression

The progression system used in Combine's chapter progression (). Replaces the fixed age-banded ladder with a reachability pool that grows with the player's discoveries, log-scaled chapters measured against cumulative finds, and crumble that tightens as the board fills.

The core idea

In banded mode the dealer samples from a fixed age window (ageSets(age) → recipes whose band is age or age+1). That window is static per age. If band 1 only has two viable 2-input combines, the dealer is stuck re-dealing them until you age up.

In chapter progression the dealer samples from a pool that expands with the player's discoveries. Early on the pool is just base ingredients plus recipes you could fire right now; as you discover things, each new find unlocks the next hop of the recipe graph.

Pool definition

known
base ingredients ∪ items the player has discovered this run
reachable recipes
recipes whose entire ingredient list is in known
one-hop-out
ingredients of recipes where ≥1 input is already in known
pool
known ∪ reachable-recipe outputs ∪ one-hop-out ingredients

The dealer only deals tiles from pool. Everything else is invisible until the frontier grows.

Chapters — log-scaled progression

Ages in banded mode were thematic eras (Big Bang, Formation, Life …) tied to recipe bands. Soft mode has no fixed bands, so "age" becomes a discovery milestone: Chapter N starts at a cumulative find count.

Threshold curve

threshold(k) = floor(10 × 1.8k-1) — exponential ramp. Chapter N always takes ~N finds regardless of KB size.

KB-size invariant. Fractions-of-KB thresholds break at large KBs — 40 000 items × 4% = 1 600 finds for Chapter 1. Log-scaled thresholds don't care how big the KB is: a 400-item theme and a 40 000-item theme both reach Chapter 5 after 58 finds. Chapters only get deeper (more remains to find past the last chapter) as the KB grows, not slower.

Helper milestones

Helpers (swap, clear, hint, shore, pluck, move) are granted at discovery milestones with the same curve as chapters but offset by ~50% so helpers fire mid-chapter. One grant mid-chapter, one at the next chapter boundary, repeat.

Dealer temperature

Already-discovered tiles get a weight of 0.2 in the sampler; undiscovered tiles weight 1.0. Discovered tiles aren't removed from the pool — they're still valid ingredients for undiscovered recipes — but they appear ~80% less often so fresh discoveries get the airtime.

Effect: the dealer feels like it's "showing you new things" even in late-game, rather than endlessly re-serving the same five tiles that were useful early.

Crumble — occupancy-scaled TTL

In banded mode crumble was "a tile outside the age window dies after 3 ticks." Soft mode has no age window, so crumble becomes a function of board occupancy: idle tiles die faster as the board fills up.

Formula

occ = filled / total
t = max(0, (occ − 0.3) / 0.7)
ttl = 60 − (60 − 20) × min(1, t)

Behaviour on a 6×6 board

100%3620Tight — declutter or lose stuff
Why occupancy, not time. A fixed timer punishes sparse boards (no reason to pressure the player) and under-pressures dense boards (player already overloaded). Scaling with occupancy means the game's pressure matches your actual situation — the player who's hoarding gets the squeeze, the player who's playing clean doesn't.

Interaction with shore

Shore marks a tile as crumble-exempt until its cell changes. Under occupancy-scaled crumble, shore becomes more valuable in high-occupancy play — you can preserve 1-2 key tiles while the rest of the board churns. This creates genuine strategic choice: do you keep this hard-won ingredient for a future recipe, or let it ride?

What this fixes vs. banded

Before (banded)

  • Band 1 has 26 outputs but only 2 real 2-input combines (H+He→star, H+He→sun).
  • 24 outputs gated behind 1-input promote.
  • Dealer cycles H/He/star endlessly.
  • Sim: 29% of collection reachable in 50 plays. 70% unreachable.

After (soft)

  • Pool grows each discovery — no artificial scarcity.
  • Progress feels earned ("I made X, now Y-making tiles appear").
  • Dealer temperature keeps variety high through the run.
  • Sim: 59–62% of collection reachable in 50 plays (roughly 2× banded).

What stays the same

  • Catalogue groupings — still show thematic eras (Big Bang, Formation, …) so the collection view reads chronologically. Soft mode changes the progression UI, not the collection UI.
  • Scoring — tier values, chain multipliers, close-call bonuses unchanged.
  • Fast mode — unaffected.
  • Daily mode — still target-list ending (state.target); soft sampler would apply if daily switched to it but current daily is independent.
  • Hold capacity — scales with state.age as before. In chapter progression state.age advances on chapter thresholds, so capacity still grows naturally.
